How SafeClaw Works

A visual walkthrough of the system — from inbox to action, and why your data stays private.

Slide 1 of 5
Your AI assistant lives on your server.
SafeClaw runs entirely inside a Virtual Private Server (VPS) that you control. Messages come in from Gmail or Slack, two AI agents process them using a private knowledge base (the Brain), then send replies and log tasks — all without your raw data touching any outside cloud.
Slide 2 of 5
Reader Agent scans every email.
A background Reader Agent fetches your Gmail inbox on a schedule, extracts contacts, company mentions, and key context from each thread, then writes structured observations into the Brain. Over time the Brain builds a rich picture of every relationship and deal in your inbox — without you lifting a finger.
Slide 3 of 5
Actor Agent drafts replies, logs tasks.
When you send a message through Slack or Telegram, the Actor Agent springs into action. It recalls relevant context from the Brain, then chooses the right action: drafting an email reply in your voice, creating or updating a task in the dashboard, or surfacing information from past conversations. One message, multiple results.
Slide 4 of 5
Your secrets never leave this box.
The VPS is a hard security boundary. Inside it: your Postgres database, Brain files, and encrypted API keys stored in a .env file. The agents only send structured API calls outward to Gmail, Slack, and Composio — no raw emails, no contact lists, no business data exits. Everything sensitive stays on hardware you control.
Slide 5 of 5
See everything the agent is working on.
Every task the Actor Agent creates or updates appears in real time on this Tasks dashboard. Filter by status, assignee, or date range, search across titles and descriptions, and click any task to expand its full history and comments. Nothing falls through the cracks — you always know what SafeClaw is doing on your behalf.
